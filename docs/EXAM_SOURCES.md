## Status vocabulary

- `VERIFIED_FULL_PUBLIC`: official PDF contains the complete 80-question paper and answer key.
- `VERIFIED_PUBLIC_10_PERCENT`: official PDF is explicitly a 10% public release; it is not a complete exam pack even when the 80-question skeleton is visible.
- `PDF_LINK_VIA_PAGE`: the direct PDF fetch was not stable in this research session; the linked official publication page contains the combined question/answer PDF.
- `UNKNOWN`: not verified; it must not be inferred during import.

## Import eligibility

- Only the two `VERIFIED_FULL_PUBLIC` rows are structurally complete public source documents.
- A `VERIFIED_PUBLIC_10_PERCENT` row must fail an 80-question completeness check and end in `REVIEW_REQUIRED`; it must never be padded, guessed, or exported as a full official exam.
- Copyright status remains restrictive even for `VERIFIED_FULL_PUBLIC`. These sources are research/import references, not permission to redistribute their text in the public app or repository.
- A locally supplied full paper may be processed privately, but official identity requires matching provenance and a verified final key. Otherwise label it `YDS-style Practice Test` or `UNKNOWN`, not `Official YDS`.
